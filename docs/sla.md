# Service Level Agreement
Acme Cloud guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime. If we miss it, you can claim service credits.
Credit tiers by measured monthly uptime: 99.0%-99.9% -> 10% credit; 95.0%-99.0% -> 25% credit; below 95.0% -> 50% credit.
Credits must be requested in writing within 30 days of the incident and are applied to the next invoice.
